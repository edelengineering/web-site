About


  • EDEL Engineering Inc. was established in 2017 by Dr.-Ing. Emir Kutluay.

  • We are active in Hacettepe Technopolis.

  • Our Mission

    By putting our academic skills into practice, we are a technology development and engineering services partner in line with the needs of the vehicle industry.

  • Our Vision

    A stable research and development partner in the vehicle and defense industry, instead of naturalise products to develop domestic products with innovative technologies.

Services

Systems Engineering Services

Mechanical design, finite element analysis, dynamic analysis, mobility and performance analysis, 3D modeling and technical drawing preparation, system engineering services.

Consultancy Services

Consultancy services on matters control, vehicle dynamics, driver/operator support systems, autonomous driving.

Training Services

Training services on matters vehicle technique, vehicle dynamics, driver support systems, control theory.

Personnel Supply

Supply of engineers and technical personnel to be run on companies' bootstrap or with remote connection.
